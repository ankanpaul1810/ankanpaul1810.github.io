# Photography Assets

## Required Images

Add 6 curated photographs to this directory with the following naming:

- `photo1.jpg` - Anticipation over reaction (Low light, motion, timing)
- `photo2.jpg` - Framing under constraint (High contrast, limited depth)
- `photo3.jpg` - Pattern recognition (Repetition, geometry)
- `photo4.jpg` - **Decision moment** (Crowd, movement, balance) - Featured with insight
- `photo5.jpg` - System boundaries (Edges, transitions, scale)
- `photo6.jpg` - Complexity to clarity (Layers, focus, resolution)

## Guidelines

**Curation Standards:**
- Professional quality only
- Clear visual intent
- Analytical framing (not artistic fluff)
- Resolution: At least 1920x1440px
- Format: JPG, optimized for web (<500KB each)

**Themes to Consider:**
- Architecture & geometry
- Urban systems & infrastructure
- Light & shadow patterns
- Motion & timing
- Constraints & boundaries
- Complexity resolving to clarity

**DO NOT Include:**
- Random snapshots
- Casual photos
- Over-edited images
- Generic landscapes
- Social/personal photos

**Remember:** This section signals **visual intelligence** and **systems thinking**, not photography as a hobby.
